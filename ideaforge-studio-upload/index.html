How It Works

From Idea → Website

01

Discuss

Tell us about your business, problem or idea. You don't need to understand technical terminology — just explain what you'd like your website to achieve.

02

Plan

We discuss your requirements and recommend suitable pages, features and solutions. You'll receive a quotation based on what you actually need.

03

Design

We turn your idea into a visual website experience that fits your business and brand.

04

Develop

The design becomes a real, functional website — frontend, backend, database, APIs and business functionality, depending on the project.

05

Test

We test important functions and responsive layouts across different screen sizes.

06

Launch

Your idea goes live.

07

Support & Improve

After launch, additional updates, improvements and new features can be requested. Future development is quoted separately depending on requirements.
